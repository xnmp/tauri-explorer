# 836 — Browser qualification must separate refresh completion from fetch feedback

The 2,408-case all-view run on the Git tree of `dev` `1404dd22` ended with 2,370 passed, 36 skipped, and two failures. Both were test contracts, not evidence of a changed product outcome.

In `git-graph-cache-remount.spec.ts`, F5 shows “Fetched from remotes” before `GitGraphView.refreshWithFetch` calls the graph reload in its `finally` block. The test had already paginated to commit `(#1)` and waited only for that toast before paging again. Its helper could see `(#1)` in the old 750-row snapshot and finish its count poll, then the reload would replace page 0 and unmount that row before the separate visibility assertion. The failing screenshot showed intermediate rows around `#485` instead of the oldest row. Wait for a new `git_log` request and for the old `(#1)` row to detach before asking pagination to reach the oldest commit. Do not require the page-0 head to be visible at that point: the scroller can retain its prior offset and virtualize the head out of view. The final assertion still requires the oldest commit to become visible.

In `zoom-positioning.spec.ts`, the graph-row midpoint at 130% zoom was x=987.7 in a 1280px viewport. The 269px CSS-wide menu could not fit there, so the app correctly clamped its rendered left edge to x=919.9, leaving the right edge inside the viewport. The test interpreted the 67.8px clamp as cursor conversion drift. Click the left quarter of the row and assert that the rendered menu has room to open without clamping before measuring cursor alignment. Preserve the product's keep-on-screen behavior.

A second full all-view run passed 2,371 cases, skipped 36 and failed the existing WebKit thumbnail-cache assertion once. Its setup waited only for the first `<img>`, then captured a count of three micro thumbnails. Each of the three image tiles subsequently gained a full-resolution `<img>`, so the final count was six. The observed placeholder-flash count was zero and the screenshot showed the three images. Wait for all three full-resolution layers to finish loading before recording the baseline count; this also gives the duplicated-tab cache case its stated loaded-thumbnail precondition. The corrected thumbnail-cache file passed 60/60 headless WebKit repeats with two workers.

The original full-suite failures are the pre-fix reproduction. After the test changes, the zoom case passed 20/20 headless Chromium repeats. The graph case passed 30/30 headless WebKit repeats with two workers, the full-suite concurrency. A four-worker stress run passed 16/20; four cases stalled at the earlier command-palette focus step before reaching the graph refresh and do not test this fix. Full all-view qualification and final `dev` integration remain separate gates.
