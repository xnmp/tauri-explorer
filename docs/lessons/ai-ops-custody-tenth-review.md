# AI custody must follow OS completion and artifact authority

Cancellation is a request, not evidence that execution failed. Kill the owned
process tree, reap the child, and preserve a natural zero exit plus drained bytes.
Both process paths share the reaper decision; Windows explicitly terminates its
job with nonzero code 1 before closing the job handle. A previously observed
natural nonzero exit remains a process result rather than being relabeled Cancelled.

A successful nonempty image stdout validates independently of diagnostics.
Recovery can use its already-published identity even when diagnostics are later lost. Missing, corrupt,
unreadable or nonregular stderr must not block stdout recovery or delivery.
Quota may shrink only after independent file/entry barriers; when they fail,
retain at least the prior charge, which can exceed the original manifest length.
Explicit collection still removes damaged diagnostic entries.

Recapture retires old inputs before retrying capture. Artifact rows must remain
collectable through their own released status even when the operations row is
absent; use an optional operation join. Removal also enumerates orphaned
package custody directly. Do not collect unexplained active orphan output merely
because its admission row is missing.

Compatible retention authorizes adoption; it does not prove provider admission.
After replacement status returns null, fence the original consumer operation ID,
require another null answer, and atomically recheck owner, ownership epoch,
revision, acknowledgement and absence of admission before terminal settlement.
Leave a released preparation tombstone to reject late capture/admission.
Automatically revisit admissionless work after the ordinary attention timeout.

Frontend immutable identity includes a host-incremented ownership epoch. A later
epoch authorizes owner adoption; an old epoch cannot overwrite it even with a
higher event revision. Registration cannot supply a nonzero epoch.

The model must call production reservation and native admission before metering
refusal. Expected fences and generation changes are not model preconditions.
Provider-original events need an actual durable admission; consumer preparation
and idle events may precede it. Preserve invocation truth across lost receipts.
Count actual transitions, including shared-consumer admissions, rather than
synthetic applied increments. Count physical objects by native file identity so
hard links share a charge while separate equal-byte files do not.

The eleventh review clarifies the capture contract: RAM-only bytes cannot survive
a crashed process. Persist no completion identity until the named streams and
entries are durable; every native and ProcessSpool completion uses one shared
Store barrier. Stream stdout while reading, and never truncate that exact stream
again at completion. Before identity publication, interruption settles silently
and releases charges. Once identity exists, conservation and delivery remain
mandatory. Paid invocation truth remains independent of recoverable success.
See `ai-ops-durable-completion-ordering.md` for the regression boundaries.
